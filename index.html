About Us

Woldia Comprehensive Specialized Hospital

15+ Years of Excellence

Your Trusted Partner for Advanced Medical Care

Woldia Comprehensive Specialized Hospital stands as a beacon of health in the Amhara region, providing high-standard medical interventions, trauma care, and inpatient services supported by modern technologies and devoted professionals.

  • State-of-the-art diagnostic imaging and modern laboratory facilities.
  • 24/7 Emergency, Trauma, and Critical Care units.
  • Expert multidisciplinary clinical consultants and specialists.

Our Vision

To be a leading comprehensive specialized hospital in East Africa renowned for excellence in clinical care, medical education, cutting-edge research, and patient-centered health services.

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, high-quality, compassionate, and equitable specialized healthcare services, while fostering professional medical training and promoting community wellness.

Leadership Message

"Our patients are our utmost priority. Every day, our dedicated physicians, nurses, and staff strive to bring healing, hope, and superior clinical expertise to everyone who walks through our doors."

Dr. Tadesse Mekonnen

Chief Executive & Medical Director

Medical Services

Our Comprehensive Departments

Specialized medical fields equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and compassionate experts.

Cardiology

Advanced cardiac diagnostics, ECG, echocardiography, and heart failure management.

Neurology

Specialized care for brain disorders, stroke recovery, epilepsy, and nervous system conditions.

Orthopedics

Comprehensive bone, joint, ligament replacement, trauma surgery, and physical therapy.

Pediatrics

Dedicated neonatal and infant care, pediatric immunizations, and adolescent medicine.

Dental Care

Full-service oral health, restorative dentistry, extractions, and cosmetic treatments.

Ophthalmology

Advanced vision correction, cataract surgery, glaucoma treatment, and optical dispensary.

Laboratory

Automated pathology, microbiology, hematology, and rapid biochemical diagnostics.

General Surgery

Modern modular operating theaters equipped for complex visceral and laparoscopic procedures.
